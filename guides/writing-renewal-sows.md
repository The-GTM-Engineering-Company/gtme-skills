# SOW Completion Audit & Renewal Scope

Check what an engagement delivered against its previous SOW, then draft the next scope from verified outcomes, remaining work, and new priorities. Useful for GTME delivery owners and contractors preparing a renewal.

**Public skill package:** [SOW Completion Audit & Renewal Scope](https://github.com/The-GTM-Engineering-Company/gtme-skills/tree/main/skills/writing-renewal-sows)

No GitHub account, invitation, or username sharing is needed to download the package. Each operator still needs their own authorized access to the relevant services.

## 1. Before you start

- The previous delivery scope, ideally as an extract without pricing, payment, or signature sections.
- Authorized delivery evidence: artifacts, trackers, commitments, recaps, and relevant call or message records.
- The engagement cutoff date, proposed renewal term, and known priorities.
- A private workspace; optionally the exact document destination for the approved draft.

## 2. Set up with your assistant

Open a private client workspace and paste:

```text
Install the writing-renewal-sows skill from:
https://github.com/The-GTM-Engineering-Company/gtme-skills

Read skills/writing-renewal-sows/SKILL.md and its references.
Use the supplied scope extract and approved delivery evidence. Keep
commercial terms and private audit material out of shared outputs.
Identify missing sources or decisions. Start with an audit and local
renewal-scope draft; do not alter a contract or send anything.
```

Keep the complete skill folder and its reference files together. Your assistant can install it for Codex or Claude Code, or read it directly from the downloaded repository.

## 3. Run the first scoped task

```text
Audit each deliverable in the previous SOW. Verify completion against
primary evidence and later corrections. Identify carryover, work
outside the original scope, and documentation gaps without double
counting. Draft the next scope from the evidence and stated priorities.
Mark inaccessible or contradictory evidence as unverified.
```

## 4. What you should receive

- A completion audit with evidence for delivered, partial, in-flight, not-reached, superseded, and unverified items.
- A carryover register and deduplicated account of additional work.
- A renewal-scope draft with deliverables, dependencies, ownership, and open decisions.

See the [synthetic example](https://github.com/The-GTM-Engineering-Company/gtme-skills/blob/main/skills/writing-renewal-sows/examples/audit.md) for the expected shape. It contains invented data.

## 5. Review and continue

Review delivery status and proposed scope with the engagement owner. When the draft is approved, specify which document sections to update. Preserve fees, payment terms, signatures, and other commercial/legal sections. This workflow does not execute contracts or change billing.

## Practical limits

A stale tracker is not proof of completion, and missing source access is not proof of non-delivery. This skill supports delivery analysis and drafting; the operator remains responsible for contract review.
