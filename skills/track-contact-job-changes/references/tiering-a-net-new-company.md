# Fit for a net-new company

Evaluate the operator's configured criteria only. A blank criterion is not a filter. Each criterion produces pass, fail, or unknown with evidence and freshness.

Typical criteria include employee range, geography, industry/business model, revenue, and relevant technology or business context. Use the same explicit scoring or all-required-pass policy across the batch.

- **Fit:** the configured acceptance rule passes and every required criterion has enough evidence. Propose account creation only after a complete CRM match check.
- **Non-fit:** a required exclusion or rejection rule is evidenced. Propose the configured skip/flag treatment.
- **Needs review:** any required criterion is unknown, or no meaningful criteria were defined/evaluated. Do not silently promote an incomplete profile to fit, and do not claim missing evidence proves non-fit.

An approved contact-role update can be appropriate even when no account is created. Make the text-field and association decisions explicit; do not invent an association to a nonexistent account. Preserve the prior employer in history.

See [company matching](company-matching-rules.md). Record pass/fail/unknown reasons beside the proposed action so the operator can inspect the judgment.
