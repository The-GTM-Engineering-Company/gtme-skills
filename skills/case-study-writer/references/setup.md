# Case-study setup

Record these choices in the private workspace, not the shared package:

- Client identity and which facts may be disclosed.
- Engagement state: completed, in progress, or partially delivered.
- Approved evidence paths/links and excluded sources.
- Requested persona editions and named/anonymized variants.
- Author/agency name and style. Default to The GTM Engineering Company only for GTME-authored work; use another brand when the operator requests it.
- Desired length and destination: local draft, an existing document, or a specific Notion parent page.
- Whether publishing is part of the requested task.

Ask only for gaps that affect the result. If downstream results were not measured, use verified operational outcomes without implying revenue or meetings.
