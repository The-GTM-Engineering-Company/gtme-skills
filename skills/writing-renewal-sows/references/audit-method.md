# Audit method

## Evidence passes

Group related SOW bullets so they can be reviewed together. First derive provisional statuses from primary artifacts and contemporaneous records. Then perform a separate challenge pass: look for later corrections, paused workflows, missing acceptance, and statements that contradict completion. Finally sweep approved recaps, commitments, messages, and calls for additional work, and check the merged audit for omissions and duplicates.

Use independent reviewers/subagents only if available and authorized by the task environment. Otherwise perform the same passes sequentially. Do not invent a workflow runtime or claim an independent review occurred when it did not.

## Status rules

| Status | Evidence required |
| --- | --- |
| DELIVERED | Named artifact, verified operational result, or documented acceptance for the actual bullet |
| PARTIAL | Identify the completed and unfinished portions |
| IN FLIGHT | Recent evidence of active work; describe remaining scope |
| NOT REACHED | Evidence of deferral or a sufficiently covered corpus supporting that work did not begin |
| SUPERSEDED | Document the decision/change and what replaced the original scope |
| UNVERIFIED | Required evidence is missing, inaccessible, or contradictory |

Give every row a source location and date. A tracker status alone is not evidence. A relaunch may mean earlier delivery stopped working; investigate it rather than counting two wins. Do not treat invisible/private execution as delivered without evidence.

## Coverage and adjudication

Record which expected sources were inspected and which were unavailable. Adjudicate every material correction and explain the final status. Deduplicate additional work by the underlying outcome, not wording. Preserve open documentation/acceptance gaps in the carryover register.

The internal audit can describe problems candidly. A client-facing renewal should be constructive while keeping material scope and delivery facts accurate. Do not hide an unresolved item by calling it completed.
