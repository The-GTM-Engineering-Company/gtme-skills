# Output template

## Completion audit

- Engagement and cutoff date.
- Sources inspected and coverage limitations.
- Status counts, including unverified items.
- Per-deliverable table: ID, original commitment, final status, evidence/date, completed portion, remaining portion, and next decision.
- Deduplicated work outside the SOW.
- Documentation/acceptance gaps and carryover register.

## Renewal scope draft

- Background: original problem, evidenced current state, and reason for the next phase.
- Engagement objective and agreed term.
- Workstreams: concrete deliverables, ownership, acceptance evidence, dependencies, and exclusions.
- Carryover: woven into the relevant workstreams and traceable to the audit.
- Standing support/reactive work, where requested, with scope boundaries.
- Open operator decisions.

Exclude pricing, payment details, signatures, and legal boilerplate from this draft template. Preserve those sections when editing an existing commercial document.
